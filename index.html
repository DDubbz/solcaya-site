Common questions about bespoke DJ mixes

Common questions

Everything you need to know

How much does a custom DJ mix cost?

The studio offers three commission tiers. Sol at $197 delivers a 45-minute bespoke mix with private taste consultation, MP3 and WAV masters, two revisions, and seven-day delivery. Caya at $397 extends to 75 minutes with full curation, custom intro and outro, stems included, and revisions until right. Solcaya starts at $750 for two-plus hours or multi-mix event suites.

How long does a bespoke mix take?

Sol commissions are delivered within seven business days. Caya commissions receive priority handling and are completed within five business days. Solcaya timelines are bespoke by design — the studio agrees a dedicated delivery schedule with you during your private consultation. Regardless of tier, the delivery clock starts only when both your completed brief and the 50% non-refundable deposit are received.

Can I request an NDA?

The studio takes discretion seriously. An NDA is available on request for any tier and is included as standard on every Solcaya commission. Names are never shared with third parties, commissions are never publicised, and all work is delivered under strict confidentiality. For event planners requiring it, white-label delivery ensures no Solcaya attribution reaches your client.

What genres do you work with?

The studio works across all genres and has composed mixes spanning deep house, R&B, Afrobeats, disco, techno, ambient, hip-hop, jazz, and world music. Every commission is curated entirely by hand — no algorithms, no auto-playlists. Your private taste consultation defines the palette: the studio selects tracks that match the exact mood your moment demands, then sculpts them into a single continuous performance.

Do you deliver white-label for planners?

The studio provides white-label delivery as standard on Caya and Solcaya tiers. Your commission arrives fully branded for your client — no Solcaya attribution appears unless you explicitly request it. This is designed for luxury event planners and venues who need seamless delivery under their own brand.

What formats do I receive?

Every commission includes mastered MP3 and WAV files — the industry-standard formats for playback on any system. Caya and Solcaya tiers additionally include stems, giving you or your sound engineer individual track control for fine-tuning. Clean edits are available on request for any tier, and the studio can deliver radio-ready versions where required.
