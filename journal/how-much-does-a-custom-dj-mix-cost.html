How much does a custom DJ mix cost?

Solcaya offers three commission tiers. Sol at $197 delivers a 45-minute bespoke mix with private consultation, MP3 and WAV masters, two revisions, and seven-day delivery. Caya at $397 extends to 75 minutes with full curation, custom intro and outro, stems included, and revisions until right within your brief. Solcaya starts at $750 for two-plus hours or multi-mix event suites, with white-label planner delivery and a dedicated timeline.

What's included at each tier?

Every commission begins with a private taste consultation — a conversation about the records you love, the mood you want, and the moment your mix must carry. From there, the studio curates, composes, and refines by hand until the result feels inevitable.

How long does each commission take?

Sol commissions are delivered within seven business days. Caya commissions receive priority handling and are completed within five business days. Solcaya timelines are bespoke by design — the studio agrees a dedicated delivery schedule with you during your private consultation. Regardless of tier, the delivery clock starts only when both your completed brief and the 50% non-refundable deposit are received.

Why does a private consultation come standard?

A Solcaya mix is not a playlist. It is a single continuous performance built around your taste, your story, and the exact mood your moment demands. The consultation is where that begins — the studio asks about the records you love, the energy you want, the moments the mix must carry. That conversation shapes every track selection, every transition, every pacing decision. Without it, the mix would be generic. With it, the result could only be yours.

Can I request an NDA?

The studio takes discretion seriously. An NDA is available on request for any tier and is included as standard on every Solcaya commission. Names are never shared with third parties, commissions are never publicised, and all work is delivered under strict confidentiality. For event planners requiring it, white-label delivery ensures no Solcaya attribution reaches your client.

What formats will I receive?

Every commission includes mastered MP3 and WAV files — the industry-standard formats for playback on any system. Caya and Solcaya tiers additionally include stems, giving you or your sound engineer individual track control for fine-tuning. Clean edits are available on request for any tier, and the studio can deliver radio-ready versions where required.
